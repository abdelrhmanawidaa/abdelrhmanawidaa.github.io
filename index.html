01 About

I'm a DevOps and MLOps engineer with three years of production experience building and operating cloud infrastructure for software and AI systems across AWS and GCP. At Valeo I operate in a dual DevOps/MLOps capacity while owning internal products end to end — identifying process gaps, designing the solution, and delivering it solo from proof of concept through production deployment. That includes an LLM-powered error intelligence system now serving 100 teams, and MCP servers deployed into private cloud infrastructure — including private GitHub integration — a capability very few freelance or in-house engineers have actually shipped.

I care about the gap most AI projects fall into — between a working demo and a system with auth, monitoring, cost control, and reliability wrapped around it — and I close it myself, without handoffs.
