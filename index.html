About me

I'm a DevOps and MLOps engineer at Valeo, sitting between the infrastructure and machine learning teams and owning internal products end to end — finding the process gap, designing the fix, and shipping it to production myself. The largest of those is an LLM error intelligence system that now triages production errors for 100 engineering teams, built on Vertex AI Model Garden with the gateway, auth, and monitoring around it.

The half of the work I care most about is the unglamorous half: observability, cost control, rollback paths, and Terraform that lets someone else rebuild the system without me in the room. I've deployed MCP servers into private, firewalled cloud environments — including against a private GitHub instance — and built the Kubernetes, Helm, ArgoCD, and MLflow tooling that 30 development teams ship through every day.

I'm based in Cairo, I studied Computer Science at El Shorouk Academy, and outside of work I'm usually rebuilding something I already built once, properly this time.
